Privacy policy

How Lonar Trading Company Limited handles information you share through this website.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by Lonar Trading Company Limited, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 8080526). In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Lonar Trading Company Limited.

What information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you send an enquiry, we may receive:

  • your name, company name, email address and phone number
  • details of the materials you are interested in, such as product category, specification, quantity and delivery location
  • anything else you include in your message

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • to reply to your enquiry and prepare quotations
  • to manage and deliver any order you place with us
  • to keep records of our business dealings, as required by law

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Services that help us run this website

Some trusted services may process information when you use this website:

  • Your own email service, if you send the enquiry form or write to us by email
  • An email form service, if the enquiry form is set up to send by email
  • Google Maps, which shows our location on the contact page
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver the website’s fonts and a small script, and may receive your device’s IP address in doing so
  • Our website host, which keeps standard technical logs

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting business relationship, and then only as long as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.
