02Paper & board expertise

Specialist support for paper and packaging buyers

Paper and board are our core trade. Choosing the wrong grade leads to breakages, poor print and wasted money, so we help you get it right.

Grade selection

We help you choose the right grade for the job, weighing print process, strength, conditions of use and cost.

Alternatives & optimisation

The most expensive grade is not always the right one. We propose equivalent options that meet the need at a better price.

Supply coordination

We coordinate orders, documentation and follow-up with suppliers and logistics partners through to delivery.

03How we work

From request to delivery, in four steps

A clear, simple process, so you always know where your order stands.

Request a quote
01
  1. 01

    Tell us your requirement

    Share the application, grade or grammage, format (reel or sheet), quantity and delivery location. Not sure of the specification? Tell us what the material needs to do.

  2. 02

    We source and propose options

    We identify suitable grades, including equivalent alternatives, and come back with a clear proposal on quality, price and timing.

  3. 03

    We confirm specification and terms

    We agree the final specification, quantities, pricing and delivery terms with you before anything is ordered.

  4. 04

    We coordinate through to delivery

    We manage the order, paperwork and follow-up with suppliers and logistics partners, and keep you informed until the material arrives.
