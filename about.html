Built on reliable supply

Lonar Trading Company is a diversified trading and procurement company that provides reliable sourcing solutions to a wide range of industries, including construction, hospitality and manufacturing.

01Who we are

A trusted partner for dependable supply

Lonar Trading Company Limited (RC 8080526) is based in Abuja, FCT. With a strong commitment to quality, efficiency and customer satisfaction, we serve as a trusted partner for businesses seeking dependable supply chain support and uninterrupted access to essential materials.

We trade and supply cartons and packaging materials, paper and raw materials for carton manufacturing, tools and spare parts, housekeeping and cleaning supplies, construction and building materials, and industrial consumables. This broad portfolio lets us meet diverse procurement needs across sectors, keeping operations running and sourcing cost-effective.

02Our mission

Our mission and vision

To provide exceptional trading services that make transactions seamless, foster sustainable growth and build lasting partnerships through integrity, reliability and innovation.

Our vision

Leading trade in Nigeria and beyond

To be the leading trading company in Nigeria and beyond, recognised for excellence in sourcing, distribution and customer service, and contributing significantly to the economic development of Nigeria.

Who we serve

Businesses that need dependable supply

  • Manufacturing: raw materials, packaging, tools, spare parts and consumables
  • Hospitality: housekeeping, maintenance and operational supplies
  • Construction: building materials and procurement support
  • Businesses that need reliable import and sourcing services
03What we do

Local trading, importing and expert advice

We cover the full buying journey, from finding the right product and supplier to clearing customs and delivering to your door.

  • Local trading: sourcing and distributing products within Nigeria
  • Import trading: international sourcing, customs clearance and freight forwarding
  • Subsidiary support: dedicated trading services for associated companies
  • Consultancy: advice on import and export rules, markets and supply chains
  • Paper & board expertise: helping customers choose the right grade
