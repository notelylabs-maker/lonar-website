Built on reliable supply

Lonar Trading Company Limited buys, sells and trades paper, board and packaging materials. We make sourcing simpler and more dependable for the businesses that convert, print and package across Nigeria.

01Who we are

A Nigerian trading company for paper, board and packaging

Lonar Trading Company Limited is registered in Nigeria (RC 8080526) to buy, sell and trade industrial materials. Our focus is the paper and packaging industry: the printing papers, corrugated papers and packaging boards that manufacturers, converters and printers rely on every day.

We sit between producers and buyers. That position lets us see both sides of the market, so we can match the right material to the right application, at the right price, and keep supply moving.

02What we do

We source, supply and coordinate

Our work covers the full buying journey, from understanding what a customer needs to making sure the material arrives as agreed.

  • Trading printing papers, corrugated papers and packaging board
  • Helping customers choose the right grade for their application
  • Proposing equivalent alternatives when a grade is scarce or costly
  • Connecting mills and suppliers with commercial buyers in Nigeria
  • Coordinating orders, documentation and follow-up through to delivery

Explore our services

03Our approach

How we approach materials trading

Paper and board are technical products. Small differences in grade change how a box performs or how a page prints. We trade with that in mind.

Start with the application

Before we talk about price, we ask how the material will be used: the machine, the product, the load and the conditions it faces.

Compare options honestly

We set out the realistic choices, including equivalent grades and alternatives, with the trade-offs explained in plain terms.

Follow through

Agreeing the order is only half the job. We stay involved until the material is delivered and the customer is satisfied.

04Our philosophy

Our business philosophy

A good supplier does more than sell you material. It helps you avoid the wrong one, and it is still there for the next order.
