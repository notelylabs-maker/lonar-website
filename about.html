Built on reliable supply

Lonar Trading Company Limited is a dynamic and reliable trading company based in Abuja, Federal Capital Territory. We specialise in local and international trade, offering sourcing, procurement and distribution solutions to our associated companies and a diverse range of clients across the region.

01Who we are

A trusted trading partner in the Nigerian market

Lonar Trading Company Limited (RC 8080526) buys, sells, imports and distributes goods for businesses in Abuja and across Nigeria. Our core trade is paper and packaging: the printing papers, corrugated papers and packaging boards that manufacturers, converters and printers rely on every day.

We also source and import industrial equipment and spare parts, raw materials for manufacturing, construction materials, hospitality items, consumer goods and electronics, and general merchandise. Our commitment to quality, efficiency and customer satisfaction makes us a partner businesses can depend on.

02Our mission

Our mission and vision

To provide exceptional trading services that make transactions seamless, foster sustainable growth and build lasting partnerships through integrity, reliability and innovation.

Our vision

Leading trade in Nigeria and beyond

To be the leading trading company in Nigeria and beyond, recognised for excellence in sourcing, distribution and customer service, and contributing significantly to the economic development of Nigeria.

Who we serve

Businesses that need dependable supply

  • Manufacturers and industrial businesses that need raw materials
  • Converters, printers and packaging producers
  • Businesses looking for reliable import and export services
  • Retailers and wholesalers in Abuja and surrounding areas
03What we do

Local trading, importing and expert advice

We cover the full buying journey, from finding the right product and supplier to clearing customs and delivering to your door.

  • Local trading: sourcing and distributing products within Nigeria
  • Import trading: international sourcing, customs clearance and freight forwarding
  • Subsidiary support: dedicated trading services for associated companies
  • Consultancy: advice on import and export rules, markets and supply chains
  • Paper & board expertise: helping customers choose the right grade
