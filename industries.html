The industries we serve

We provide reliable sourcing to three core sectors: manufacturing, hospitality and construction. Each depends on uninterrupted access to essential materials, and that is what we deliver.

Sector 01

Manufacturing

We supply raw materials, packaging products, tools, spare parts and industrial consumables that support production processes and operational efficiency.

Sector 02

Hospitality

We provide housekeeping supplies, maintenance materials and operational products that help hotels, resorts and hospitality businesses maintain high service standards.

Sector 03

Construction

We deliver construction materials, equipment-related supplies and procurement support for residential, commercial and industrial projects.

—Within manufacturing

Paper and packaging specialists

Paper and board are a core part of our trade. We supply the businesses that turn these materials into cartons, boxes and printed products.

  • 01Carton manufacturers

    Paper reels, folding boxboard, duplex and triplex grades that print well, crease cleanly and run efficiently.

  • 02Corrugated box makers

    Imported kraftliner, white-top testliner and fluting to build board with the strength your customers need.

  • 03Printing companies

    Offset, coated and label papers suited to your press, finish and run length.

  • 04Paper bag manufacturers

    Kraft papers matched to the strength, finish and printability your bags require.

  • 05Packaging producers

    Cartons, packaging materials and paper supply planned around your production schedule and volumes.

Why reliable supply matters

When materials arrive on time, production keeps running, guests stay happy and projects stay on schedule.
