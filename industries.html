The industries we supply

Paper and board are the starting point for much of what Nigeria prints and packages. We supply the businesses that turn these materials into finished products.

Duplex · Triplex · FBB · SBS

Carton manufacturers

Folding cartons have to print well, crease cleanly and stand up on the shelf. The right board reduces waste on the line and keeps finished cartons looking sharp.

Kraftliner · Testliner · Fluting

Corrugated box makers

Box strength comes from the liners and fluting you choose. Matching grades to load, humidity and handling helps boxes protect their contents without over-specifying.

Woodfree · Coated · Label papers

Printing companies

Paper affects colour, sharpness, drying and press speed. The right sheet for your press and finish keeps runs efficient and results consistent.

Paper & board supply

Packaging producers

Production plans depend on material arriving when expected. We coordinate supply around your schedule and volumes so your lines keep running.

Kraft papers

Paper bag manufacturers

Paper bags need the right balance of strength, stretch and printability. We help you find kraft grades that suit your bag type and finish.

Heavy liners & boards

Industrial packaging

Industrial goods travel further and face rougher handling. Heavier liners and boards help packaging protect products through transport and storage.

—Why it matters

Why the right material matters

The right material keeps machines running, boxes strong and print sharp. The wrong one costs time, money and customers.
