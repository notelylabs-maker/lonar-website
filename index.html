01Who we are

Who we are

We connect paper mills and material suppliers with the Nigerian businesses that print, convert and package, and help them buy the right grade the first time.

Lonar Trading Company Limited is a Nigerian company that buys, sells and trades industrial materials, with a focus on paper, board and packaging. We work between producers and the businesses that depend on them. We learn what each application demands, find materials that meet it, and coordinate supply so production keeps moving.

The wrong grade is expensive. It shows up as breakages, poor print results, boxes that fail in transit and last-minute supplier changes. We bring commercial and technical judgement to every order, so you can buy with confidence.

04How we work

From specification to delivery, in four steps

Send us what you need. We’ll come back with a clear proposal.

01
  1. 01

    Tell us your requirement

    Share the application, grade or grammage, format (reel or sheet), quantity and delivery location. Not sure of the specification? Tell us what the material needs to do.

  2. 02

    We source and propose options

    We identify suitable grades, including equivalent alternatives, and come back with a clear proposal on quality, price and timing.

    We confirm specification and terms

    We agree the final specification, quantities, pricing and delivery terms with you before anything is ordered.

  4. 04
