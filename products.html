01

Paper reels & corrugated papers

Imported paper reels and the raw materials carton manufacturers need, including kraftliner and white-top testliner. Box performance depends on load, humidity and handling, so we help you pick the right grade for each use.

Typical applications

  • Shipping cartons
  • Food & beverage boxes
  • Retail-ready packaging
  • Produce boxes
  • Industrial packaging
Kraftliner
Liner made mainly from virgin fibre, for high strength and stiffness in demanding boxes.
Testliner
Liner made from recycled fibre, a cost-effective choice for a wide range of boxes.
White-top liners
White-top testliner and kraftliner, with a bright top layer for better print on branded and retail boxes.
Fluting
The wave-shaped middle layer that gives corrugated board its cushioning and stacking strength.
Semi-chemical fluting
High-performance fluting with strong crush resistance, for heavy loads and humid conditions.

Grammage, reel width and availability vary by grade and supplier. We confirm exact specifications with every quotation.

02

Packaging board

Carton boards for folding cartons and consumer packaging. We focus on what matters on your line: printability, stiffness, creasing and clean conversion.

Typical applications

  • Food cartons
  • Pharmaceutical cartons
  • Cosmetics & personal care
  • Consumer goods
  • Printed displays
Duplex board
Recycled board with a white coated top, widely used for folding cartons of everyday consumer goods.
Triplex board
Like duplex, with a white back as well as a white top, for cartons where both sides are seen.
Folding boxboard (FBB)
Virgin-fibre board with excellent stiffness and print quality, popular for food, pharmaceutical and cosmetic cartons.
Solid bleached board (SBS)
Premium bleached board with a bright, smooth surface for high-end packaging and graphics.

Grammage, sheet size and availability vary by grade and supplier. We confirm exact specifications with every quotation.

03

Printing papers

Papers for commercial, publishing and label printing, supplied in reels or sheets. We help you choose by print process, finish and the result you expect on the finished job.

Typical applications

  • Books & publishing
  • Commercial print
  • Brochures & catalogues
  • Labels
  • Forms & stationery
Uncoated woodfree (offset)
Bright, smooth uncoated paper for offset printing of books, forms, stationery and general commercial work.
Copier & laser paper
Woodfree paper made to run cleanly through office printers and copiers.
Coated papers
Double-coated gloss, matt or silk papers for sharp images and strong colour in brochures, catalogues and magazines.
Label papers
Papers coated on one side, with a printable face for wet-glue and self-adhesive labels.

Grammage, sheet size or reel width, and availability vary by grade and supplier. We confirm exact specifications with every quotation.
